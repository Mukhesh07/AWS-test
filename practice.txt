Bees play a vital role in our global ecosystem by pollinating flowering plants, which allows crops, fruits, and
nuts to grow and feed both wildlife and humans. As they travel from flower to flower gathering nectar, they carry
microscopic pollen grains on their fuzzy bodies, transferring them and fertilizing the plants in the process. Without
these tiny workers, many of the foods we eat every day—such as apples, almonds, berries, and coffee—would become extremely
rare or disappear entirely. Protecting bee populations through safe gardening habits and reduced pesticide use is
essential for keeping our natural environment healthy and food supplies secure.
